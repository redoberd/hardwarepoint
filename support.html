Frequently Asked questions

In this block you can find answers to common questions about website, components, compatibility.

How I can choose the right CPU for my motherboard?

Check if your motherboard supports this processor, and whether it supports the new processor's socket

How much RAM I need for working in 4 windows at the same time?

If you're just using a browser, 16GB of RAM will be enough, but if you're doing creative work, it's best to go for 32GB of RAM so that the system runs much faster

How do I choose the graphic card for gaming?

It all depends on your monitor's resolution and the games you play; if you're playing new, powerful games, it's best to go for a graphic card with at least 6GB VRAM, as most games require 6 or 8GB.

What the difference between SSD and HDD?

HDDs are older and cheaper, but can offer more GB or even TB of storage. SSDs are newer and faster, but they're also more expensive
